Sun control

Tension screens and window coverings keep harsh sun and glare off the glass, so rooms stay cooler and more comfortable through the summer.

Every screen is made to measure, and you choose how it runs: by hand, by motor or fully automated.

How we work

We bring in your architect and designers from the first meeting and keep you informed at every stage, from the first sketch to the last panel.

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your building, how you want it to look and what you want to spend.

  2. Proposal

    You receive a detailed proposal with materials, a timeline and a cost estimate.

  3. Planning

    We finalise the design with your architect and schedule the work.

  4. Installation

    Our team installs the facade, with regular quality checks along the way.

  5. Completion

    We hand over the finished facade and ask for your feedback.
